Use the ratatui skill. # Eval 06: Handle terminal resizing

## Task

language: rust

Build a TUI with a two-column layout (list + detail) that gracefully degrades
to single-column below 80 columns and shows "terminal too small" below 40x8.

## Acceptance criteria

- No panic on resize from 200x60 to 1x1
- Layout switches at 80 columns
- Too-small message shows required and actual dimensions
- Content is not lost on resize (selection preserved)
- Tested at (1,1), (39,7), (40,8), (79,24), (80,24), (200,60)
